    Booking an appointment
    
        
      
          
    1. Open Book and search for the patient by name, phone number or patient number.
      2. 
          
    2. Click their row to choose them. If they are new, use + New patient — the system gives them a number such as PAT-2026-000006.
      3. 
          
    3. Pick the dentist and the treatment. The treatment sets how long the visit takes, so you never type an end time.
      4. 
          
    4. Choose the date and start time, then press Book this appointment.
      5. 
          
    5. Give the patient the appointment number from the confirmation.
      6. 
        
    

        
    If the slot is already taken
    
        
    Two appointments can never overlap for the same dentist. Instead of just refusing, the system offers the next free times — click one to book it straight away.
    
        
    A ten-minute gap is always kept between a dentist's appointments, so a time right after another visit may still be refused.
    

        
    After the visit
    
        
    The dentist marks the appointment Completed, or No-show if the patient did not arrive. Only a completed visit can be billed.
    

        
    Producing a bill
    
        
      
          
    1. Open Billing and enter the appointment number.
      2. 
          
    2. The total is the dentist's consultation fee plus treatments, less any discount the patient qualifies for. Discounts apply to treatments only.
      3. 
          
    3. Press Print receipt.
      4. 
        
    
        
    Each appointment is billed once. Trying again will tell you so rather than creating a second bill.
    

        
    Your session
    
        
    Sessions end after 20 minutes of inactivity — the sidebar shows how long is left. Please use Sign out when you leave the desk.
