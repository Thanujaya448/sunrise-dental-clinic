    Help

    Step-by-step instructions for clinic staff.

    Booking an appointment

    1. Open Book, then search for the patient by name, phone number or patient number.
    2. Click their row to select them. If they are not listed, use New patient — the system allocates a number such as PAT-2026-000006.
    3. Choose the dentist and treatment. The treatment sets how long the appointment lasts, so you never enter an end time.
    4. Pick the date and start time, then press Book appointment.
    5. Give the patient the appointment number shown in the confirmation.

    If the slot is already taken

    Two appointments can never overlap for the same dentist. Rather than simply refusing, the system offers the next free times — click one to book it instead.

    A ten-minute gap is always kept between a dentist's appointments, so a time immediately after another appointment may still be refused.

    After the visit

    The dentist marks the appointment Completed, or No-show if the patient did not arrive. Only a completed appointment can be billed.

    Producing a bill

    1. Open Billing and enter the appointment number.
    2. The total is the dentist's consultation fee plus treatments, less any discount the patient qualifies for. Discounts apply to treatments only.
    3. Press Print receipt.

    Each appointment can be billed once. Trying again tells you so rather than creating a second bill.

    Sessions

    Your session ends automatically after 20 minutes of inactivity; the timer in the top right shows when. Always use Sign out when you leave the desk.

    Common messages

    • Your session has expired — sign in again.
    • Your role does not permit this action — the task belongs to another role. A receptionist cannot complete visits; a dentist cannot produce bills.
    • Five failed sign-ins lock the account. Only the Administrator can unlock it.
